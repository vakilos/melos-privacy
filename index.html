Melos – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Melos is an Android app for managing song guides and setlists for live gigs and rehearsals, with a companion web app, Melos Companion, for preparing songs on a computer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use them. The short version: your data stays on your devices, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Who is responsible

Vakilos, developer of Melos. Contact: vakilos.apps@gmail.com.

What Melos stores on your device

Everything you enter or attach is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage: songs, titles, artists, keys, tempos, lyrics, notes (with the pictures, sketches and scores you put in them), setlists and projects, shows (their dates, times, places and Google Maps links, fees, whether they were paid, and your notes) and their rehearsals (dates, times, places, notes and the songs to rehearse), attached files (scores, PDFs, images, scans, audio, text notes), your annotations on scores, YouTube links you add to songs, and the app's settings. The developer has no access to any of it.

The buttons on a show (Directions, Open in Maps, Add to calendar) hand the place or the date to the Maps or calendar app on your device; Melos sends nothing itself. A place you share to Melos from Google Maps is only read on your device.

Calendar. A single show or rehearsal goes to your calendar through your calendar app's own form, which needs no permission. Only if you press "Add all to calendar" on a show's rehearsals does Melos ask for access to your calendar. It then reads the list of your calendars, so you can choose one, and writes the rehearsals into it as events; adding them again updates those same events. Melos does not read your other events, and nothing from your calendar leaves your device. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Reminders. If you allow notifications, Melos shows a reminder on your device the evening before a show or a rehearsal. The reminders are made on your device from your own shows; nothing is sent to any server for them. You can switch them off in Melos (Menu → Reminders) or in your phone's settings.

If your device's own backup is switched on, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup. That is a feature of Android, controlled in your device settings, not something Melos sends anywhere.

Optional: sync with your Google Drive (Melos Pro)

If you unlock Melos Pro and choose to sign in with Google, Melos copies your songs, setlists, shows (with their fees, notes and rehearsals), notes (with their pictures), lyrics, annotations and, if you switch them on, your audio and score files to a private application folder in your own Google Drive. That folder is reserved for Melos: it is not shown among your normal Drive files, and other apps cannot read it. Melos asks only for this permission (the "application data folder" access, drive.appdata). Your data goes directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any server of the developer.

Melos keeps the email address of the account you signed in with on your device, to show it on the account screen and to request access. Signing in is optional and Melos works fully without it.

Your Drive data is stored under Google's own terms and privacy policy. You control it: you can sign out in the app, and you can remove Melos's synced data and revoke its access in your Google Account (Data & privacy → Third-party apps and services → Melos).

Google user data and Limited Use

Melos’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain terms: Melos uses your Drive app folder only to sync your own Melos data between your devices. It does not use it for advertising, does not sell it, does not transfer it to anyone, and no human, including the developer, reads it.

Melos Companion (web app)

Melos Companion runs in your browser. Your songs, setlists, shows and attached files are kept in that browser's own storage on your computer (or in a folder you choose, if you switch on auto-save). They are not sent to the developer or to any server of the developer.

Optional sync. If you press Sync and sign in with Google, the companion copies your songs, setlists, shows, notes (with their pictures), lyrics and, if you switch it on, audio to the same private application folder in your own Google Drive that Melos on the phone uses (drive.appdata access only), and reads back what Melos on your phone put there. The data goes directly between your browser and Google. The companion keeps the email address of the account in your browser, to ask Google for the same account next time; the web version keeps no lasting login, so after an hour it asks you to continue. Google's sign-in page is loaded from Google when you use it. The same Limited Use commitment as above applies. You can sign out in the companion's Sync window, and revoke its access in your Google Account (Data & privacy → Third-party apps and services → Melos Companion).

The companion has no advertising, analytics or tracking. A YouTube link plays inside the companion only after you press Play here.

Purchases

Melos Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Melos never sees your payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether this account owns Melos Pro, and keeps that answer on your device so it works offline.

Document scanner

The "scan" function uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner, provided through Google Play services. Scanning is processed on your device. The resulting pages are saved as a PDF inside Melos on your device.

Pictures in notes

When you add a picture to your notes, Melos opens Android's photo picker, which gives Melos only the picture you choose; Melos asks for no permission to your photos and cannot see the others. A copy of the picture, made smaller, is kept with the song inside Melos on your device.

What Melos does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling, and no crash-reporting service run by the developer.
  • No account with the developer and no developer servers.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Google Play may give the developer anonymous technical reports (for example crashes and performance) for users who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google. These contain no songs or personal content.

Network use

Melos connects to the internet only to talk to Google: for sign-in and Drive sync (if you use them) and for purchases through Google Play. A YouTube link you add to a song opens in your YouTube app or browser only when you tap it.

Sharing files

When you share songs or make a backup, Melos creates a .setlist file and hands it to the app you choose (for example email or Bluetooth). A shared setlist can take its show along (date, times, place and rehearsals) if you choose; the fee and your notes are never put in a shared setlist, only in a backup of everything. What happens to the file after that is up to you and that app. Importing a file only reads it on your device.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a song or setlist in Melos removes it from your device (and from Drive at the next sync, if you use sync). Uninstalling Melos or clearing its storage removes the local data. For Drive data, see the section above.

Children

Melos is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be described in the app's update notes.
